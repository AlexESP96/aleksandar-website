Why this is different

I sit on your side of the table.

Alongside my practice I work within the HR function of a US-headquartered international company, where I assess candidates, support people processes and work daily with a distributed, multicultural workforce. Applied psychology is not only what I research — it is what I practice inside an organization every week.

Most workplace wellbeing providers study organizations from the outside. I spend my working week inside one: reviewing candidates, supporting people processes and seeing where policy meets reality across a distributed multicultural workforce. So when we discuss burnout, retention or manager capability, we start from the operational reality you actually deal with rather than a generic model.

That sits alongside a Master’s in Industrial & Organizational Psychology and doctoral research on burnout, leadership and wellbeing conducted across approximately 30 Spanish organizations.

Service packages

I take on a limited number of organizational engagements each quarter, so early conversations are the most useful ones.

Clear ways organizations can engage.

Flexible options for HR, People & Culture, Employee Experience and wellbeing teams looking for practical, psychology-informed support.

Workplace Wellbeing Workshop

A practical 90-minute session focused on burnout prevention, stress awareness, emotional regulation, communication under pressure and sustainable performance.

Leadership Wellbeing Session

A workshop for managers and team leaders covering psychological safety, difficult conversations, recognizing burnout risk and supporting sustainable performance.

When an Employee Is Struggling

A workshop for managers on the conversation they most fear: the moment an employee says something that sounds serious. Drawn from four years of frontline suicide prevention and crisis intervention work and translated into what a manager can actually do — how to notice, how to respond, what to say, where the boundary sits and when to escalate. It does not turn managers into counselors. It stops them freezing.

Employee Support Pathway

Confidential psychological support for employees navigating stress, burnout risk, relocation, workplace challenges and professional pressure.

Ongoing Wellbeing Partner

A flexible collaboration combining workshops, consultations, employee support and wellbeing initiatives over time.
